Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

What we collect

Sora does not collect personal data such as your name, email, location or device identifiers. There are no accounts, analytics or ads.

Photos and videos

Everything you shoot or import, and all filter processing, stays on this device and is never sent to a server. Deleting the app deletes the Sora album. Copies saved to Photos remain.

Permissions

Camera: taking photos and videos Microphone: recording video sound (optional) Photos: saving results and importing photos you choose Notifications: only if you turn on the golden hour reminder (scheduled on device)

Purchases

Filter pack purchases are processed by the Apple App Store (or Google Play). Sora never receives your payment details; it only checks with the store which packs you own.

Retention and deletion

Sora keeps nothing on a server. Photos and settings live only on this device and are gone when you delete them in the album or delete the app.

Children's privacy

Sora collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will say so in an app update and on this page.
