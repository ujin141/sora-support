FAQ

How do I record video?

Press and hold the shutter to record, release to stop. Up to 15 seconds.

Can I change the filter later?

Yes. Open a photo in your album and tap Edit; Sora re-develops it from the original, so quality never degrades. Videos keep the filter they were shot with.

The preview looks slightly different

The live preview is an approximation. The filter is applied exactly when you shoot, and you can compare real results on the filter screen.

Turning off or changing the date stamp

Use Date in the editor. Change the default in Settings > App settings.

Where are photos saved?

In the Sora album on this device, and also in Photos when “Also save to Photos” is on.

My packs are missing on a new device

Tap Restore purchases in the store or in Purchase history.

How do I get a refund?

App Store payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
